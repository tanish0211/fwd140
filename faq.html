STUDENT HELP CENTER

Questions?
We've got answers.

Find useful information about meal timings, menus, membership and dining services.

DINING & TIMINGS
1. What are the timings of breakfast, lunch and dinner?

Breakfast is served from 8:30 AM to 10:30 AM, lunch from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM and dinner from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM, according to the demonstration schedule on this website.

Students should check official mess notices if timings change.

2. Where can I check the daily mess menu?

Open the Home page and go to the Menu section. You can use the calendar to select a date and view breakfast, lunch and dinner planned for that day.

You can also use the dish search and meal filters to quickly find menu items.

3. Can the mess menu change during the month?

Yes. Menu items may change due to availability, operational requirements or other circumstances. The menu displayed on this website is a project demonstration and should not replace official mess notices.

4. How can I identify vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes?

Each dish has a small category marker. A green marker indicates vegetarian dishes and a red marker indicates non-vegetarian dishes in the current menu display.

MEMBERSHIP & PAYMENTS
5. What is the monthly mess membership price?

The illustrative monthly membership rates displayed on this website are ₹3,400 for the vegetarian plan and ₹3,800 for the non-vegetarian plan.

These amounts are demonstration rates and should be verified with mess management before making any payment.

6. How can I apply for a mess card online?

Open the Mess Card page, select your plan and fill in your name, student ID, email and course details. The website then displays a simulated payment screen for demonstration purposes.

This version does not process actual payments or issue official mess cards.

7. Can I apply for a mess card offline?

Yes, the demonstration process is to visit the mess manager, provide your student details, select a membership plan and complete payment through the officially available method.

After verification, ask the mess manager about membership confirmation and card collection.

STUDENT SUPPORT
8. Where can I share feedback about food or dining services?

Use the feedback form on the homepage. You can share your experience, rate it and suggest changes.

In this demo version, feedback is stored locally in the browser and is not automatically delivered to the mess administration.

9. Who can I contact for a mess-related concern?

You can visit the Contacts page to use the student support form. You can also approach the mess manager or the relevant committee representative in person.

Actual official contact details should be added once confirmed.

10. What should I do if my question is not listed here?

Scroll down to the Ask a Question section. Enter your name, email and query. Your question will be saved locally in this demonstration website.

For an urgent real-life issue, contact the appropriate university or mess official directly.
